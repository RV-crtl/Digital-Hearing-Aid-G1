# Final physical prototype — 5% mechanical rubric checklist

This checklist is for the actual assembled device. KiCad design files alone do not establish pass/fail of a manufactured assembly.

## Minimum (ALL must work)

- [ ] PCB retained positively within a custom enclosure; can lift/move the device without rattles or circuit short.
- [ ] External connectors accessible, cables restrained; intended user controls can be reached and actuated.
- [ ] Display visible through intended window **if fitted**; microphone sound inlet unblocked **if fitted**.
- [ ] No sharp exposed contacts or unsafe acoustic coupling before supervised operation.

## Meets expectations (ALL applicable functions)

- [ ] Encoder/button caps provide grip and smooth repeatable action; lid removable without bending PCB.
- [ ] Board insertion/removal supported by internal mounts and accessible screws.
- [ ] Speaker/transducer mounted with appropriate mechanical transfer surface and grille/mesh if appropriate.
- [ ] Connector alignment and clearance allow normal cables and safe handling.

## Exceeds expectations (appropriate extra quality)

- [ ] Chamfers/fillets and consistent gaps; intentional aesthetics and high-quality fabrication.
- [ ] Internally managed wiring with insulated strain-relieved routes and separated audio/power where practical.
- [ ] Permanent user-facing labels; any LEDs have suitable light pipes.
- [ ] Ergonomics checked while worn, accessible controls, no ear/skin hot spots.
- [ ] At least one purposeful mechanical feature improves function (replaceable battery cover, adjustable retention, vibration isolation, gasket, serviceable port cover); include before/after evidence.
- [ ] BOM, dimensions, enclosure drawings and revision match actual device.

## Evidence

Photograph top/bottom assembled device, PCB fasteners, closeups of connector cutouts, acoustic ports and cable paths. Log calliper results for MEC-01 (<60 mm original target), a short movement check, repeated control actuations and a controlled doff/refit. A one-day wear-duration claim needs an actual duration trial, not a one-minute demonstration.

**Do not manufacture based solely on the repository's original KiCad project:** third-party symbol/footprint libraries are unresolved; schematic ERC, PCB DRC and DFM review are outstanding.
