# Mechanical HD acceptance — physical prototype

Rubric p. 3. **This is a workshop checklist, not a claim that the enclosure has been made.**

| Gate | Confirm with physical observation | Evidence to attach |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | All PCBs secured into custom case; no exposed/loose wiring; connectors accessible; fitted controls operate; microphone port unobstructed | Internal/external photographs with scale |
| Meets | Proper encoder/button caps; smooth reliable travel; snug removable lid; PCB insert/remove without flex; transducer properly mounted/protected; plug/unplug connectors comfortably | Assembly and 20-cycle removal/reconnection test |
| Exceeds | Chamfers/fillets, strain relief, ergonomic access, intentional labelled controls; neatly routed harness; light pipe *only if LED physically exists*; fabrication with negligible defects | Closeups, ergonomic walkthrough, robust cable retention result |
| Electrical | USB connector mechanical support, no shorts/pinch points, charger/battery not misrepresented, safe transducer and wiring | DRC/ERC outputs, connector insertion tests, supply checks |

Do not print a display window if there is no display. Do not claim light pipes if the custom PCB has no confirmed status LED. If no physical button is connected (e.g., Nucleo B1 not on PCB), a 3D printed cap doesn't solve the electrical interface.

## Fit-and-finish review

Check mounting standoffs, captured nuts/appropriate screws, removable access, service loop/bend radius, microphone acoustic port, vibration-isolated transducer attachment, noise coupling, tactile feedback, label readability, cable strain relief and user skin contact. Record current iteration and observed failures.
